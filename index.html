SOLIDARGE PRODUCT

Scientific Workbench

Solidarge’s proprietary desktop environment for materials simulation workflows, powered by Nanoworks.

In development

From structure to result, without the usual friction.

The workbench is being designed around a direct workflow: build or import a structure, choose what you want to calculate, review the meaningful settings, and run through Nanoworks. Calculation inputs remain visible for advanced users.

Structure→ Scientific task→ Run→ Results

Nanoworks integration

Structure preparation, calculation settings and results come together in a desktop workflow powered by Nanoworks.

Local to HPC

The same project and job model is intended to work from a workstation to remote HPC systems.

Expert escape hatch

Native inputs, raw logs and engine-specific controls remain accessible when needed.

Reproducibility by design

ABOUT SOLIDARGE

Research-informed software engineering.

Solidarge is being built around a narrow idea: scientific software should make advanced methods easier to use without turning them into black boxes.

Our focus is the intersection of science, engineering and software—especially computational materials, semiconductor research and reproducible scientific workflows.
