SOLIDARGE PRODUCT

Scientific Workbench

A proprietary desktop environment for scientific simulation workflows.

In development

From scientific object to result, without the usual friction.

The workbench is being designed around a direct workflow: select a structure or device, choose what you want to calculate, review the meaningful settings, and run. Native engine inputs remain visible for advanced users.

Structure / Device→ Scientific task→ Run→ Results

Engine-neutral workflows

Scientific tasks stay central while compatible computational engines remain replaceable underneath.

Local to HPC

The same project and job model is intended to work from a workstation to remote HPC systems.

Expert escape hatch

Native inputs, raw logs and engine-specific controls remain accessible when needed.

Reproducibility by design

Calculation artifacts and provenance stay together in an inspectable project structure.

ABOUT SOLIDARGE

Research-informed software engineering.

Solidarge is being built around a narrow idea: scientific software should make advanced methods easier to use without turning them into black boxes.

Our focus is the intersection of science, engineering and software—especially computational materials, semiconductor research and reproducible scientific workflows.
